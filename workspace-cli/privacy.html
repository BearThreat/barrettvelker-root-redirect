Personal Google Workspace CLI access

Privacy policy

Effective September 28, 2026. This policy covers the personal Google OAuth client named “workspace cli” operated by Barrett Velker.

Access and use

The client requests Google account permissions selected on Google's consent screen. The locally installed CLI uses those permissions to carry out Barrett's own commands and authorized automations, such as reading or updating email, files, documents, spreadsheets, presentations, calendars, and tasks. Google Cloud and Pub/Sub permissions may also be requested for related automations.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored on Barrett's computer for CLI access. Google data retrieved through the CLI may be stored or processed in Barrett's own systems as needed for the command or automation he runs. This OAuth client is not offered to other users, and it does not sell Google user data. Data may be sent to a service when Barrett directs a workflow to use that service or when required to complete a requested Google API action.

Control

Barrett can revoke this client's access through Google Account connections and remove locally stored credentials. Questions can be sent to barrettvelker@gmail.com.
